Project notes

How the full project works.

A compact CNN classifies facial expressions, then the application summarizes its frame-level outputs as engagement indicators.

ⓘImportant limitation. Emotion labels are uncertain estimates from facial expressions and should not be treated as a measure of a student's attention, understanding, or wellbeing.
01

Capture

The full local app accepts a camera frame, image, or video for analysis.

02

Classify

A trained CNN predicts one of seven expression categories with confidence scores.

03

Summarize

Frame-level outputs feed session charts and classroom-level engagement summaries.
